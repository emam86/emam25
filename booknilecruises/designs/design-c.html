Questions before you book

How long is a Nile cruise between Luxor and Aswan?

Most ships sail 4 days / 3 nights from Aswan to Luxor, or 5 days / 4 nights from Luxor to Aswan. Dahabiyas take longer because they stop at smaller sites.

What is the difference between a cruise ship and a dahabiya?

A dahabiya is a small sailing boat with a handful of cabins. It moors at quieter places and feels like a private yacht. Cruise ships are larger, with pools and more facilities.

Where does a Lake Nasser cruise go?

Between Aswan and Abu Simbel, visiting Nubian temples that the Nile cruises cannot reach.
