Every 5-star Nile cruise between Luxor and Aswan visits the same temples, so the ship and the cabin you sleep in shape your trip more than anything else. This guide explains how the ships are laid out, which deck and cabin type to choose, what facilities and food to expect, and how to compare two ships that look the same on paper.

How a 5-star Nile cruise ship is laid out

Nile cruise ships are long and narrow, built for a shallow river and for the locks at Esna. Most have three or four passenger decks plus an open sun deck on the roof:

  • Lower deck: cabins close to the waterline, often with a smaller or higher window. Usually the restaurant is here too.
  • Middle decks: most of the cabins, plus reception, the lounge and bar.
  • Upper deck: the best cabins and suites on many ships, sometimes a spa or gym.
  • Sun deck: pool, loungers, shaded seating and a bar. This is where you will spend the sailing hours, watching villages, palm groves and farmland slide past.

Cabin types

Which deck should you choose?

If your budget allows, pick a cabin on a middle or upper deck. Three reasons:

  1. Views. Higher cabins look over the riverbank instead of at water level.
  2. Noise. Lower-deck cabins near the stern can pick up engine sound and vibration during sailing.
  3. Mooring. In Luxor, Aswan, Edfu and Kom Ombo, ships often tie up side by side, several ships deep. When that happens, many cabins look straight at the next ship's window. Higher decks are less affected, and the sun deck always has a view.

On the other hand, lower decks are more stable, cost less, and are fine if you only use the cabin to sleep and shower.

Facilities on board

Facilities vary from ship to ship. Most 5-star ships offer:

  • A swimming pool on the sun deck (often small, more for cooling off than swimming laps)
  • A lounge and bar, usually where the evening entertainment happens
  • Wi-Fi, which works when the ship is near towns and can be slow while sailing
  • Laundry service
  • On deluxe and super deluxe ships: a spa or massage room, a small gym and a boutique

Dining on a 5-star Nile cruise

Meals are included on a full-board cruise. Expect a breakfast buffet, lunch and dinner served as buffets or set menus, with a mix of Egyptian dishes and international food. Higher-tier ships serve more meals à la carte. Most kitchens handle vegetarian, gluten-free and other dietary needs if you tell us when you book. Drinks with meals are usually charged separately.

Evenings typically include an Egyptian-themed night: guests dress in a galabeya, and there is folklore music, belly dancing or a tanoura (whirling) show.

Accessibility

Some ships have a lift between decks, many do not, and gangways at busy moorings can mean crossing other ships. If anyone in your group has limited mobility, tell us before booking so we can choose a suitable ship and a lower-deck cabin near the reception.

Comparing two ships: a checklist

Two ships with the same "5-star deluxe" label can be very different. Ask for:

  • Year built and year of the last full renovation
  • Total number of cabins (fewer usually means calmer decks)
  • Cabin size in square metres for the category you are offered
  • Deck and window type of your actual cabin
  • Whether there is a lift
  • Recent guest photos of cabins, not only the brochure photos

Frequently asked questions

Do Nile cruise cabins have balconies?

Standard cabins have a large window, not a balcony. French balconies are common on deluxe ships, and real private balconies are mostly found on suites and on super deluxe ships.

Is the lower deck a bad choice?

Not necessarily. Lower-deck cabins are cheaper and more stable, but they sit closer to the water, can be noisier, and are more likely to face another ship when moored. If views matter to you, choose a middle or upper deck.

Is there a pool on every 5-star Nile cruise ship?

Almost all 5-star ships have a pool on the sun deck. They are usually small and are best for cooling off in the afternoon heat.

Can I choose my exact cabin?

You can request a deck and cabin type, and we confirm the category when we book. Specific cabin numbers depend on the ship's availability for your dates.
