The Aswan–Luxor stretch of the Nile is where Egypt's great river temples stand: Philae, Kom Ombo, Edfu, Karnak and Luxor, with the Valley of the Kings across the water. A 5-star Nile cruise links them in a few relaxed days. This page gives you the day-by-day plan in both directions, what is included, the optional extras worth adding and how to get to the ship.

Itinerary: 3 nights from Aswan to Luxor

This follows our 3-night Aswan to Luxor Nile cruise.

Day 1: Aswan

We meet you at Aswan airport or railway station and drive you to the ship. After lunch on board you visit the Aswan High Dam, the Unfinished Obelisk in the ancient granite quarries, and Philae Temple, dedicated to Isis and reached by a short boat ride. Dinner and overnight on board in Aswan.

Day 2: Kom Ombo and Edfu

The ship sails north to Kom Ombo, where an unusual double temple honours both Sobek, the crocodile god, and Horus the Elder. Lunch on board while sailing on to Edfu. Dinner and overnight in Edfu.

Day 3: Edfu and Luxor

In the morning a horse carriage takes you to the Temple of Horus at Edfu, the best-preserved temple in Egypt. Back on board for lunch while the ship sails through the lock at Esna to Luxor. In the evening you visit Luxor Temple, then dinner and a galabeya party on board.

Day 4: Luxor East and West Banks

After breakfast and check-out you visit Karnak Temple, then cross to the West Bank for the Valley of the Kings and the Temple of Hatshepsut at Deir el-Bahari. Afterwards we take you to Luxor airport or railway station.

Itinerary: 4 nights from Luxor to Aswan

This follows our 4-night Luxor to Aswan Nile cruise.

Day 1: Luxor East Bank

We meet you at Luxor airport or railway station and transfer you to the ship. You visit Karnak, with the Great Hypostyle Hall's 134 columns and its sacred lake, then Luxor Temple, linked to Karnak by the Avenue of Sphinxes. Dinner and an evening show on board.

Day 2: Luxor West Bank

The West Bank: the royal tombs in the Valley of the Kings, the terraced Temple of Hatshepsut and the Colossi of Memnon. In the afternoon the ship sails south through the Esna lock towards Edfu.

Day 3: Edfu and Kom Ombo

The Temple of Horus at Edfu in the morning, then sailing to Kom Ombo for its riverside temple of Sobek and Horus the Elder, beautiful around sunset.

Day 4: Aswan

Arrival in Aswan. Visits to Philae Temple, the Unfinished Obelisk and the Botanical Garden on Kitchener's Island, with free time for an optional felucca sail or a visit to a Nubian village.

Day 5: Departure

Breakfast and check-out, then a transfer to Aswan airport or railway station, or on to Abu Simbel if you have added it.

What is included

  • Cabin on a 5-star Nile cruise ship for the nights booked
  • Full board: breakfast, lunch and dinner on board
  • Guided visits to the temples in the itinerary, with an Egyptologist guide
  • Entrance fees to the listed sites
  • Transfers between the airport or railway station and the ship
  • Evening entertainment on board

Not included

  • Drinks, tips and personal expenses
  • Special tomb tickets, such as Tutankhamun's tomb or the Tomb of Nefertari
  • Optional excursions (below)
  • Flights or trains to Luxor or Aswan, and your Egypt visa

Optional extras worth adding

  • Abu Simbel day trip from Aswan: Ramses II's colossal temples near the Sudanese border, about three and a half hours each way by road. Best added at the start (Aswan to Luxor) or the end (Luxor to Aswan).
  • Luxor hot-air balloon at sunrise: the West Bank temples and the Nile from above.
  • Philae sound and light show: Philae Temple lit up at night.
  • Nubian village by motorboat: colourful houses, tea with a local family and the islands of Aswan.

Getting to the ship

Frequently asked questions

How long is a Nile cruise from Aswan to Luxor?

Usually 3 nights and 4 days. In the other direction, Luxor to Aswan, it is usually 4 nights and 5 days because the ship sails against the current.

What is the best month for a Nile cruise?

October to April, when daytime temperatures are comfortable for sightseeing. December and January are the busiest and need booking well ahead. Summer is very hot but quieter and often cheaper.

Can I visit Abu Simbel on a Nile cruise?

Abu Simbel is south of Aswan and not on the Aswan–Luxor route, so it is visited as a day trip from Aswan or on a Lake Nasser cruise. We can schedule it before or after your cruise.

Are the temple entrance fees included?

Entrance fees for the sites in the itinerary are included on our packages. Special tombs with their own tickets, such as Tutankhamun's tomb, are extra.
